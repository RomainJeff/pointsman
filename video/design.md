# Pointsman — video design

Source: the app icon (`../logo.html`, `../Resources/AppIcon.svg`).

## Colors
- Navy top `#22345C`, navy `#0D1526` — the rail yard, always the background
- Sleeper `#3A4E78` — track ties, structure, quiet borders
- Cream `#F3EBDD` — rails and text
- Amber `#F5B43C` — only the chosen route: the browser a link actually goes to

## Type
- Outfit (600 headlines, 400 body) — close to the app's SF Rounded
- JetBrains Mono — URLs, app names, percentages (departure-board details)

## Rules
- Amber means "this is where the link goes". Never decorative.
- Track motifs (sleepers, rails, points) are the structure: dividers, wipes, bars.
- No user- or company-specific content: generic links and apps.
